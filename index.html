About the project

Deltarune Widescreen is my attempt to bring Deltarune to a native 16:9 experience. The project expands rooms, adapts graphics and environments, and makes the best possible use of the additional screen space while keeping the original artistic direction and vision of Toby Fox intact. I would like to be able to release chapter 1 in the coming weeks.

This is a solo project made in my free time. I'm a 15-year-old French student working on the mod alongside school and everything else that somehow manages to consume all available hours of the day. The main technical issues have now been solved and development is progressing steadily. I'm working on the project as quickly as possible, although my available free time is obviously limited.

Other Chapters?

The goal of Deltarune Widescreen is to eventually bring the entire game to a 16:9 experience.

As the game becomes more complex over time, the widescreen adaptations will naturally take more time to develop as well. New chapters and sections require more work, more testing, and more adaptations to make sure everything still feels right.

I'm doing my best to work as quickly as possible while keeping a certain level of quality rather than rushing things out just for the sake of having them available.
